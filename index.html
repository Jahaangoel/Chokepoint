The 2026 Hormuz crisis

A fifth of the world’s oil passes through one strait. Since February, it has barely been open.

Six sea lanes carry most of the oil that powers the world economy. This atlas shows where they are, what happens when one closes, and which shares in India and the US win or lose.

Click a strait to zoom in. Dots move along each route; figures are oil flows before the war, in million barrels a day (EIA).
