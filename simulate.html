      How to read this table

      Sensitivity is how much the stock has tended to move for a 1% move in Brent. −0.40 means it usually falls about 0.4% when oil rises 1%. The grey range is a 95% confidence interval: if it crosses zero, the link is weak.

      “2026 crisis” uses daily prices since the war began. “Normal times” uses three years of weekly prices. A supply shock like Hormuz tends to hurt more than ordinary oil moves, so the crisis numbers are usually the better guide here.
