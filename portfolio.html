Chokepoint Risk Score

What the score means

The score asks one fixed question: if Brent jumped 50%, how much would this portfolio be expected to lose? Each 2% of expected loss adds one point, up to 10.

For each stock it uses the more painful of two estimates — its behaviour in normal times and during the 2026 crisis — so the score leans cautious. A portfolio that would gain is labelled oil-hedged and scores 0.
